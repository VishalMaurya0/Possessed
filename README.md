## 🩸 Overview

**Possessed** is a **co-op multiplayer horror survival escape game** where 2–4 players enter a haunted forest and house to perform **rituals** that can either **exorcise** or **doom** them.  
Teamwork, communication, and courage are your only weapons.

Developed with **Unity (Netcode for GameObjects)**, the game blends **exploration, puzzle-solving, and psychological horror**.

## 🕯️ Core Gameplay Loop

1. Explore the haunted **house** and **forest** for clues and ritual items.  
2. Perform **3 correct rituals** out of 8 possible.  
3. Manage your **fear meter** — panic too much and risk possession.  
4. Avoid **ghost hunts** and **stalking dolls**.  
5. Rely solely on **voice chat** — no text, no HUD markers.  
6. Kill the ghost... or die trying.  

---

## 💀 Unique Selling Points (USP)

- 🩸 **Co-op Ritual System** — Choose 3/8 effects to kill the ghost (wrong ones will kill you).  
- 🧠 **Fear Meter** — Impacts vision, control, and sanity.  
- 👁️ **Creepy Doll Mechanic** — Dolls freeze when stared at but attack unseen.  
- 🔥 **Safe Point** — Deploy a team-safe zone for temporary protection.  
- 🕯️ **Possession System** — Get possessed and turn against your team.  
- 🎙️ **Voice Chat Only** — True co-op immersion.  
- ⚙️ **Procedural Intensity** — Every hunt and ghost behavior is different.

## 🎮 How to Build / Run

> ⚠️ For developers and testers only.

1. Clone the repository.  
2. Open in **Unity 2025.x**.  
3. Ensure **Netcode + Multiplayer SDK** are installed.  
4. Open `Scenes/Development/House.unity`.  
5. Press **Play** → Choose **Host / Client** in Multiplayer Center.
